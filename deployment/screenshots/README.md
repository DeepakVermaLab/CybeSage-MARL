# Paper Screenshot Set

These figures were captured from the locally served CybSAGE-MARL research console at a 1600-pixel publication viewport.

## Suggested captions

1. `01_system_overview.jpg` — **CybSAGE-MARL research-console overview.** The interface exposes the evidence boundary, held-out CAGE-4 reward, paired evaluation design, prototype policy metrics, explanation coverage, and the trajectory-to-explanation methodology chain.
2. `02_recorded_simulation_seed58_episode2_step12.jpg` — **Recorded CAGE-4 multi-agent playback.** Held-out seed 58, episode 2, step 12 shows simultaneous defender actions across five zones, the selected profile, per-step reward trace, and exact environment commands.
3. `03_explainability_step06.jpg` — **Prototype evidence-grounded explanation view.** The selected isolation action is paired with its inferred regime label and retrieved MITRE ATT&CK evidence; the panel explicitly separates synthetic prototype evidence from the recorded CAGE-4 profile trace.
4. `04_evaluation_evidence.jpg` — **Evaluation and ablation evidence.** Mean CAGE-4 rewards are shown for the retained profile and six executed baselines, with paired bootstrap confidence intervals and the separate synthetic-task component ablation.
5. `05_resources_reproducibility.jpg` — **Resource and reproducibility profile.** The console reports measured wall-clock runtime, artifact footprint, represented source paths, static deployment characteristics, and the absence of instrumented energy measurements.

Do not crop away the evidence-boundary labels when using these screenshots in the manuscript.
